# Enclosure Specifications & Layout

The Haddacerra Gateway enclosure is engineered for mechanical durability, component alignment, thermal dissipation, and for a clean fit on the RC N1-3 series RC's.

---

## 1. Physical Dimensions & Form Factor

* **Dimensions:** 150 mm × 85 mm × 24 mm

* **Form Factor:** Compact low-profile box designed for portability and ease of use.

---

## 2. Material & Fabrication

* **Material:** PA12S-HP Nylon.

* **Fabrication Method:** HP Multi Jet Fusion (MJF)

* **Post-Processing:** Surface-dyed finish for wear resistance and aesthetics.
