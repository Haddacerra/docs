## DJI Aircraft and Controller Compatibility

Haddacerra interfaces with the control signals of the DJI RC-N1, RC-N2 and RC-N3 controllers. The original controller retains its normal radio link to the aircraft.

Aircraft compatibility follows the stanard DJI Compatibility chart.

The integrated enclosure is designed to replace the phone mount area on the RC-N1, RC-N2 and RC-N3 controllers, accommodating the gateway as part of the controller assembly.

An additional magnetic smartphone/tablet holder is provided, allowing a smartphone or tablet to be mounted while the controller's mounting arm is occupied by the gateway.

DJI smart controllers with built in screens are not supported by the current enclosure design, which relies on the phone mount area for integration.


## Crossfire Integration

Dedicated Crossfire connection pad allow users to connect a compatible Crossfire control setup directly to the gateway.
